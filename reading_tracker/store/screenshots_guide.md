# ReadPp Store Screenshots Guide

Use a clean demo library with realistic Spanish copy and polished covers.

## Recommended Screens

1. **Onboarding**
   - Screen: `Tu viaje lector, en un solo lugar`.
   - Purpose: first impression and product promise.

2. **Home**
   - Show a current reading hero with cover, progress, metrics, goal, and recent activity.
   - Purpose: premium reading dashboard.

3. **Biblioteca**
   - Show grid with several books and filters.
   - Purpose: personal collection.

4. **Book Detail**
   - Show cover hero, progress card, metadata, and sessions.
   - Purpose: individual book experience.

5. **Progress**
   - Show reading challenge, activity card, and quick access cards.
   - Purpose: tracking and continuity.

6. **Insights**
   - Show hero insight and curiosity cards.
   - Purpose: discovery and reading profile.

7. **Perfil / Ajustes**
   - Show theme selector and preferences.
   - Purpose: customization without account setup.

## Capture Notes

- Capture both Burgundy and Forest if store asset count allows.
- Avoid empty states in primary screenshots except onboarding.
- Use readable progress values and human dates.
- Keep status bar clean.
- Review text at Play Store thumbnail size.
