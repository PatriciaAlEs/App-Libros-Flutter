# ReadPp Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-06

ReadPp is a personal reading tracker.

## Data Stored By The App

ReadPp stores reading data locally on your device, including:

- Books added to your library.
- Reading status and progress.
- Reading sessions, pages, minutes, and optional notes.
- Ratings and reading goal preferences.
- Theme preference.
- Onboarding completion state.

## Accounts

ReadPp v1.0 does not include user accounts, login, remote profiles, or authentication.

## Remote Sync

ReadPp v1.0 does not sync your library or reading activity to a remote server.

## Third-Party Services

ReadPp can search book metadata using Open Library when you use the add-book search. Search terms are sent to Open Library to return book results.

## Analytics And Ads

ReadPp v1.0 does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

## Data Deletion

You can delete books and reading data from inside the app where those actions are available. Uninstalling the app removes local app data according to the operating system behavior.
